Who Am I

A versatile product designer blending user research, product management strategy, web development, and UX mentorship.

I am Akbar Hari Mukti, a Product Designer with 5+ years of experience designing scalable digital products across fintech, crypto, enterprise platforms, smart mobility, and IoT.

I combine deep user insights, business strategy, and structured design thinking to create intuitive experiences that solve real problems. My career spans not only interface design, but also product strategy and technical execution.
